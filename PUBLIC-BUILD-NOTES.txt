Rules:
- No series/news/event/artist/merch content is stored in HTML or JS snapshots.
- New published Series, News, Events, Artists and Merch appear from the API without new HTML files.
- Detail pages are generic route shells handled by vercel.json rewrites.
- Only logo.png is a local image asset.
- Editable images come from the MEDIA registry / Google Drive.
- Hidden ad containers remain off unless database ad settings enable them.
- Series/Episode records with Auto_News enabled are supported as automatic news updates.
- Episode releases are automatically included in the Events calendar renderer.
- CMS is intentionally NOT included in this ZIP.
